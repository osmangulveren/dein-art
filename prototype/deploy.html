Put dein.art on the testnet

This deploys dein.art's contracts to Sepolia, Ethereum's test network, from your own wallet. Test ETH has no value and is free from a faucet; nothing here touches real money.

  1. Have a walletMetaMask or Rabby in this browser.
  2. Get free Sepolia ETHFrom any Sepolia faucet. About 0.02 test ETH is plenty for both contracts.
  3. DeployYour wallet asks you to confirm one transaction for each contract.

Splits

Pays a film's support out to its people

Source: contracts/DeinArtSplits.sol. Whoever releases a film records its split; support is paid out in the same transaction, minus a flat fee of test ETH that goes to the wallet that deployed the contract. Nothing is held.

Page claims

Records which wallet claimed which page

Source: contracts/DeinArtClaims.sol. A wallet can only claim its own page, so nobody can claim or block a page for someone else.
