Put dein.art on the testnet

This deploys the page-claims contract to Sepolia, Ethereum's test network, from your own wallet. Test ETH has no value and is free from a faucet; nothing here touches real money.

  1. Have a walletMetaMask or Rabby in this browser.
  2. Get free Sepolia ETHFrom any Sepolia faucet. About 0.01 test ETH is plenty: deploying needs roughly 0.002, a claim far less.
  3. DeployYour wallet asks you to confirm one transaction on Sepolia.

The contract's source is contracts/DeinArtClaims.sol. A wallet can only claim its own page, so nobody can claim or block a page for someone else.
