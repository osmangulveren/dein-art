On-chain artists

Artists whose work already lives on a blockchain do not need to start from zero. These pages are built from public on-chain records and are unclaimed: the artists have not joined dein.art. An artist claims a page by signing a message with the wallet that created the work. No password, no upload, no fee.

How claiming works

1. The record is already publicThe collections, the number of pieces and the wallet that created them are read from the chain.
2. The artist signs a messageSigning proves who holds the wallet. It is free and it is not a transaction.
3. The page is theirsThe signature is checked against the wallet on record. If it matches, the page is claimed.
